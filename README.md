### Hi, I'm Marcelo Machado 👋

Professor and researcher in Computer Science, currently at the Department of Computer Science of the
Federal University of Juiz de Fora (UFJF), Brazil. PhD in Informatics from UNIRIO.
Before joining UFJF, I spent six years as a Research Scientist at IBM Research Brazil.

**What I work on**

- **Search and chat as learning processes**: how people learn while searching the Web or talking to chatbots and generative AI, and the cognitive biases involved
- **Personalized learning**: adaptive curriculum sequencing with metaheuristics and learning analytics
- **Semantic Web**: knowledge graphs, Wikidata, SPARQL and integration of heterogeneous knowledge bases
- **Generative AI and LLMs**: language models as sources of structured knowledge and for question answering over knowledge bases
